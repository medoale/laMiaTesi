This prevents **Use-After-Free (UAF)** vulnerabilities. Invaliding the handle first ensures that any subsequent attempts to use that handle will fail early (e.g., returning `SEC_E_INVALID_HANDLE`) rather than operating on memory that is in the process of being deallocated.

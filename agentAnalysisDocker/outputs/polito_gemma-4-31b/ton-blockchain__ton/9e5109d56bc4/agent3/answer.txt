`s.size()` is of type `size_t`. On a 64-bit system, the maximum value of `size_t` is $2^{64}-1$, which is `ffffffffffffffff` in hexadecimal. The resulting string `ffffffffffffffff\r\n` is 18 characters long, including the null terminator. A buffer of 64 bytes is sufficient to hold this string, and thus no buffer overflow is possible here.
